⚖️ Operations & Regulatory Compliance

Fleet model, headcount plan, ministry requirements, and seasonal demand management. Full compliance with MoCI, MoL, MOT, Baladiya, and PDPPL.

Ministry Compliance ChecklistAll Ministries
🏛️

MoCI — April 2026 Platform Guidelines

Commercial Registration (CR) mandatory. Full commission disclosure. Merchant price autonomy. Grievance portal with 5-day SLA resolution.

👷

MoL — Summer Outdoor Work Ban & WPS

Outdoor ban Jun 1–Sep 15, 10:00AM–3:30PM. Violation: QAR 5,000/rider fine + closure risk. WPS: rider salaries via HSBC/QNB by 7th of each month.

🚐

MOT — Vehicle Licensing & GPS Telematics

MOT commercial transport license + annual inspection mandatory. GPS telematics: 90-day data retention. Delivery box: R-value ≥1.8 insulation standard.

🍽️

Baladiya — Food Hygiene Certification

Valid Baladiya Health Cards (annual). Monthly cold-chain box inspection for pharma/perishables. Food handler training required.

🏦

QCB & PDPPL Data Privacy (Law 13/2016)

NAPS + Apple Pay + Google Pay + Himyan required. All user data on Azure Qatar Central in-country servers. No cross-border transfer without MOTC approval.
