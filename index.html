⚖️ Regulatory Compliance & Operations

Full compliance framework: Qatar ministry requirements, summer work ban protocol, vehicle licensing, payment regulations, and data privacy law.

Ministry Compliance ChecklistRegulatory Blueprint
🏛️

MoCI — April 2026 Platform Guidelines

Commercial Registration (CR) mandatory. Commission disclosure to all merchants. Price autonomy per MoCI ruling. Grievance portal: 5 business-day resolution SLA.

👷

MoL — Summer Outdoor Work Ban & WPS

Outdoor ban Jun 1–Sep 15: 10:00AM–3:30PM. All outdoor delivery must cease or face QAR 5,000/rider fine and closure. WPS: rider salaries by 7th of month.

🚐

MOT — Vehicle Licensing & GPS Telematics

All vehicles need MOT commercial transport license and annual inspection. GPS telematics mandatory with 90-day data retention. Delivery boxes: R-value ≥1.8.

🍽️

Baladiya — Food Hygiene Certification

Valid Baladiya Health Cards for all food delivery riders (annual renewal). Cold-chain boxes: monthly Baladiya inspection for pharma and perishable deliveries.

🏦

QCB & PDPPL Data Privacy (Law 13/2016)

NAPS + Apple Pay + Google Pay + Himyan integration required. All user data on Azure Qatar Central in-country servers. No cross-border transfer without MOTC approval.
